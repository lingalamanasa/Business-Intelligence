Stackly Business Intelligence is an elite autonomous analytics platform and strategic architecture firm. We partner with chief data officers, enterprise architects, and executive suites to unify fragmented data stacks, deploy autonomous AI copilots, and eliminate query friction.

OUR HERITAGE

From Columnar Query Labs to Global Intelligence Platform

Founded in 2018 by veteran distributed database architects and AI research fellows, Stackly was built to eliminate the latency, SQL hallucinations, and compute egress costs of legacy BI.

Today, Stackly powers real-time query acceleration across 240+ Fortune 500 enterprises, analyzing over 10 billion rows daily with sub-second P99 response times.
